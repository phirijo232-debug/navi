    Settings

    Network access for this BMF server. Private keeps it on the local network. Public makes it reachable on the internet. Protected routes still require a signed-in device.

    Loan interest rates

    Rates apply to new requests in the selected store. Existing loans keep their recorded interest. Custom-date terms stop accruing term interest on the due date.

    The daily rate is added only after day 28, up to the requested due date. It scales with the 1-week rate. Grace/default charges follow their separate existing policy.
